[h1]Automated Public Transport Planning – Development Version[/h1]
[b]This is the testing build.[/b] It is updated automatically with every change, before the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3823739383]Release Version[/url]. It may be unstable. For normal play, use the Release Version.

[b]Never subscribe to both versions.[/b] They are the same mod and will conflict.

[h2]Developer tools are included[/h2]
Besides the read-only diagnostics, this build contains a save round-trip test that [b]creates and removes test bus lines in the loaded city[/b]. Its buttons stay disabled until you tick the confirmation box in the mod settings. Only use it on a copy of your save.

Automatic bus line planning is not available yet.
