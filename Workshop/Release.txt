[h1]AI Gaming[/h1]
[b]Early preview (0.1.0).[/b] What if an AI could play Cities: Skylines? AI Gaming aims to connect a local AI model or a cloud AI service to the game, so you can watch an AI build a city, let it assist you while you play, or hand it the controls.

[h2]What this version does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shows the project status in the mod settings, in English and Traditional Chinese.
[*]Can write a short summary of your city – population, funds and in-game date – to the game log.
[*][b]It does not control the game yet, makes no network connections and asks for no API keys.[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Where it is going[/h2]
[list]
[*]First, reading the city in a form an AI can understand.
[*]Then, AI suggestions that you review before anything happens.
[*]Later, carefully limited AI control that you switch on yourself.
[*]Working with the other Modpack mods, such as AI Improve and Automated Public Transport Planning, instead of duplicating them.
[/list]
