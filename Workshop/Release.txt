[h1]Automated Public Transport Planning[/h1]
[b]Early preview (0.2.0).[/b] Planning a good bus network by hand means studying where people live, work and travel, then placing stops and lines that actually serve them. This mod aims to do that planning for you: it will propose bus lines from your city's real demand, show every line as a preview, and build nothing until you accept it.

[h2]What this version does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Surveys your city's roads, stops and transit network when a city loads.
[*]Offers read-only diagnostic tools in the mod settings: measure planning costs, capture a world snapshot, build the route graph and list bus models. Results go to the game log.
[*][b]Read-only: it does not change your city or your save.[/b]
[/list]

[h2]Not available yet[/h2]
Automatic bus line planning is still in development. This preview lets the groundwork be tested on real cities of every size before planning arrives.
