These are directions, not release dates. Every step ships only when it is safe for your save.

## AI Improve

**Now:** available on the Workshop with 25 switchable features.

**Next:** in-game screenshots for this guide, and continued tuning based on player reports.

## Automated Public Transport Planning

| Stage | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Read-only network survey and diagnostics | **Now** · 0.2.0 |
| Demand analysis and candidate city-bus lines | Being tested in the Development Version |
| Line previews that you review and accept before anything is built | Then |
| Other public transport types (trams, metro, trains, ferries and more), one at a time | Later |

It currently covers regular city buses only.

## AI Gaming

| Stage | Status |
| --- | --- |
| City summary written to the log | **Now** · 0.1.0 |
| Reading the city in a form an AI can understand | Next |
| AI suggestions that you review | Then |
| Limited AI actions that you switch on | Later |

## All mods

The Modpack mods are built to work together. As they grow, AI Gaming will use what the other mods can do instead of reimplementing it, and no two mods will take over the same job.

Follow progress in the [change log](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/change-log/).
