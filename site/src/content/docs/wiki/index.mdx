Welcome to the guide for the Cities: Skylines 1 Modpack by Kingsley. Start with **[Getting started](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/getting-started/)** if this is your first visit.

## The mods

| Mod | Status | What it does today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AI Improve](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/overview/) | Available on the Workshop | Vehicles, services and citizens react to congestion, free capacity and nearby needs. 25 switchable features. |
| [Automated Public Transport Planning](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/transport/overview/) | Early preview 0.2.0 | Surveys your network and offers read-only diagnostics. Automatic bus planning is in development. |
| [AI Gaming](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-gaming/overview/) | Early preview 0.1.0 | Writes a short city summary to the game log. AI play is in development. |

The mods work on their own and are designed not to conflict when you use them together.
