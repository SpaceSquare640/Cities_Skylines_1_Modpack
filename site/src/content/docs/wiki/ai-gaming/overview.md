AI Gaming will connect a local AI model or a cloud AI service to Cities: Skylines, so you can watch an AI build a city, let it advise you, or hand it the controls. Version **0.1.0** is the first early preview.

## What 0.1.0 does

- Shows the mod's version and status in its options, in English or Traditional Chinese (following the game's language).
- **Write city summary to log** — writes one line with the in-game date, population and funds of the loaded city to the game log.

It does not control the game, makes no network connections and asks for no API keys.

## Three ways to play (planned)

| Mode | The AI | You |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watch AI play | Reads the city, decides and builds. | Watch, and pause or take over at any time. |
| AI assists you | Suggests what to do, with reasons. | Review each suggestion and decide. |
| AI takes over | Acts within the limits you allow. | Choose what it may do and keep the final say. |

## How it will keep you in control

1. **Observe** — first, read the city in a form an AI can understand.
2. **Suggest** — then, suggestions you review before anything happens.
3. **Act, with permission** — later, limited actions that you switch on yourself.

AI Gaming is designed to work through the other Modpack mods, such as AI Improve and Automated Public Transport Planning, rather than duplicating them. API keys for cloud services are designed to stay out of your save and the game log.
