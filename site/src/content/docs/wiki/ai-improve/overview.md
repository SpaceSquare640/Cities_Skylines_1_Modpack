AI Improve gives the vehicles, services and citizens of your city better judgement. In the base game many choices ignore what is happening right now: a car keeps its route through a jam, trains crowd one platform while another is empty, and several fire trucks answer one small fire. AI Improve fixes these one behaviour at a time, and every behaviour has its own switch.

## What it improves

| Area | In short |
| --- | --- |
| Road traffic | Cars, taxis, trucks, local and intercity buses reconsider their route when the road ahead is congested. |
| Rail | Metro and intercity trains choose a free platform and can reroute around congestion. |
| Aviation and shipping | Airport gates, passenger helicopter pads and harbour docks are assigned by real occupancy; airports refuse landings and departures in thunderstorms. |
| Emergency services | Fire responses per building are capped, idle fire vehicles check nearby fires first, emergency helicopters stay grounded in thunderstorms. |
| Sanitation | Garbage trucks and hearses with room to spare collect from nearby buildings first. |
| Citizens | Fewer car trips toward congested destinations, and more taxi use. |
| Optional | Emergency vehicle rerouting, shorter stops when nobody is waiting, and spacing out bunched vehicles. These start switched off. |

The [feature list](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/features/) describes each one, with its default and adjustable values.

## Requirements

- **[Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402)** is required.
- **UnifiedUI** is optional; it adds an in-game panel. See [Using AI Improve in game](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/in-game/).
- Enable only one copy of AI Improve, whether it comes from Steam Workshop or a manually installed GitHub Development build.

## Languages

English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Français, Español and Русский. The mod follows the game's language unless you choose another one in its options.

## Known limit

Aircraft rerouting while taxiing does not trigger yet: measured taxiway congestion never reaches the level it reacts to. Its switch exists, but expect no effect for now.
