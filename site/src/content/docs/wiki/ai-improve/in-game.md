---
title: Using AI Improve in game
description: "Change AI Improve settings without leaving your city, and use the empty-vehicle scans."
---

You can change AI Improve in two places. Both edit the same settings, so a change in one shows up in the other straight away.

## Options page (always available)

From the main menu or the pause menu, open **Options** and select **AI Improve**. This works with or without other mods. The [settings guide](/Cities_Skylines_1_Modpack/wiki/ai-improve/settings/) explains the page.

## In-game panel (with UnifiedUI)

If you also use the **UnifiedUI** mod, AI Improve adds a button to the UnifiedUI toolbar while a city is loaded. Click it to open the **AI_Improve - Feature Toggles** panel:

- Every feature is listed with an **[On] / [Off]** label. Click one to switch it.
- The panel and the options page share the same settings.
- Without UnifiedUI the button does not appear; use the options page instead.

## Scanning for empty intercity vehicles

The panel also has two buttons: **Scan for empty intercity trains** and **Scan for empty intercity buses**. They look for intercity trains or buses travelling with nobody on board. If any are found, the mod tells you how many and asks before removing them. Nothing is removed without your confirmation.
